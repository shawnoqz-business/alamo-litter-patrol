PRIVACY POLICY

HOW WE HANDLE YOUR INFO

Short version: we only collect what we need to serve you. We don't sell your data.

Last updated: September 17, 2026

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect your name, address, email, phone number, ZIP, and details about your cat(s) and litter setup. We need all of it to deliver service.

How we use it

  • To schedule and deliver your service
  • To send appointment reminders, photo confirmations, and skip-week prompts
  • To keep a payment method on file and bill you monthly for completed visits
  • To respond to your questions or feedback

Who we share it with

Nobody, with these exceptions: our payment processor, Stripe (which stores your card details; we never see or store your card number), our customer database (Airtable), our website host (Netlify), our internal notifications (Slack), and any government or legal entity that requires it by law. We don't sell your data. We don't share it for marketing.

Your rights

You can ask us anytime to see what we have on file, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@alamolitterpatrol.com and we'll handle it within 7 days.

Cookies & Advertising

Our website uses minimal cookies, mainly so Stripe can secure the card form and to remember settings. On some pages, we also use the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel, which lets us see when someone visits from one of our ads or mailers and, if they don't book right away, show them a follow-up ad later. This doesn't affect your service in any way, and we don't sell your data to anyone. You can control ad personalization through your Facebook/Instagram account settings or your browser's tracking preferences.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@alamolitterpatrol.com.
