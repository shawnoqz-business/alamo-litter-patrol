HOW IT WORKS

IN THREE EASY STEPS

From sign-up to your first clean box, here's exactly what happens.

STEP 01

Pick Your Service

Choose what fits your home: box swap (we drop a clean pre-filled box, take the dirty one), Litter-Robot cleaning (we deep-clean your Robot at our facility and return it within 3 days), or scoop-only. Then pick home entry or porch service.

STEP 02

We Come Weekly

Same day, every week. We text you a heads-up the day before. Reply SKIP anytime to skip a week, no charge. On service day, we're in and out fast: we swap your box, scoop, or pick up your Litter-Robot for cleaning, then send a photo confirmation.

STEP 03

Live Your Life

Let us take care of the scooping. No more lugging heavy litter. No more guilt. Cancel anytime. No contracts, no fees.

Pick The Service That Fits You

🟧 Box Swap

We bring a clean, pre-filled stainless steel box, take the dirty one, and you're done. Litter is included. The dirty box gets deep-cleaned and sanitized at our facility and returned the following week.

🤖 Litter-Robot Cleaning

If you own a Litter-Robot (or similar automatic box), we pick it up, deep-clean it at our facility, and return it within 3 business days. While yours is away, we leave a standard loaner box so your cat is never without.

🧹 Scoop Only

Don't need a swap? We'll come scoop your existing box. Home entry required. No setup fee.

🐱 Litter Delivery (Add-On)

Need litter too? Add litter delivery to your service day, and we bring our premium clay or source your preferred brand. Available as an add-on to any active service.

🏠 Home Entry vs. Porch Service

Home entry is our standard for box swap and Litter-Robot service: we come inside, swap the box, leave. If your cat has porch access and you'd rather not have anyone come in, porch service knocks $5 off every visit. Scoop services always require home entry.

📱 Communication

The day before your service, you get a text. Need to skip a week, no problem, reply SKIP to skip that week with no charge.

🎖️ Senior Discount

10% off the entire monthly bill for customers 65+. Verified on first visit, no paperwork needed.

Take The Next Step

Fill out the form. We'll confirm your first service day within 24-48 hours.
